My agent card

My agent card | SG Weather Watch | finished Mon 19 Oct 2026, 23:15 SGT
1. The job: Emails the latest Singapore weather forecast (sky, min/max temperature, humidity, wind, UV) to a student who commutes to SMU (me), each time BBC Weather updates it, so I know whether to carry an umbrella.
2. The trigger: RSS by Zapier "New Item in Feed" finds a new item in BBC Weather's 3-day Singapore feed (checked every 15 minutes on the free plan).
3. The tools: RSS by Zapier: New Item in Feed (reads the feed only). Gmail: Send Email (to me only). No other apps or actions.
4. It must never: email anyone but me. The Gmail "To" field is typed in as my own address, never mapped from the feed, and CC and BCC are left empty.
5. Where a person checks: I (the student) read each email when it arrives and check it against Zap history and the BBC Weather page every evening, until I turn the Zap off after class on Wed 21 Oct.

Written before the Zap's first run. I set the 6-hour cadence of my original idea aside: Zapier's free Schedule trigger has no "every 6 hours" option, and fetching weather on a schedule would need a third step.
