My agent card

My agent card | SG PSI & Weather Update | finished Tue 20 Oct 2026, 01:25 SGT (v3)
1. The job: Emails a Singapore commuter (me, a student going to SMU) the official 24-hour PSI and PM2.5 for North, South, East, West and Central, plus the current temperature and the 6-hour forecast, so I know whether to wear a mask or carry an umbrella.
2. The trigger: Schedule by Zapier "Every Hour" (Asia/Singapore). A filter lets only the 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 runs continue.
3. The tools: Schedule by Zapier (trigger). Filter by Zapier. Code by Zapier (JavaScript that only reads three public NEA endpoints on data.gov.sg: psi, twenty-four-hr-forecast, air-temperature). Email by Zapier: Send Outbound Email (to me only).
4. It must never: email anyone but me. The "To" field is typed in as my own address, never mapped from data, and CC and BCC are left empty.
5. Where a person checks: I (the student) read each email when it arrives. Once a day I confirm the Zap is still on, scan Zap history for anything Errored, and compare one email with NEA's figure for the same hour, until I turn the Zap off after class on Wed 21 Oct.

Two honest notes. (1) I set the job on Wed 14 Oct, 14:30 SGT ("PSI updates for Singapore, a trigger every 6 hours, one email to myself"), but I only wrote this five-line card on 20 Oct, after the build, so it was not written before the first run as the brief asks. (2) The brief asks for one trigger and one action on the free plan. My Zap has four steps, because Zapier's schedule has no "every 6 hours" option (so a filter is needed) and an email step cannot fetch data by itself (so a code step is needed). These run on Zapier's 14-day trial, and the editor warns that the Zap "uses paid features". My earlier one-trigger, one-action attempt, SG Weather Watch (RSS → Gmail), followed the rules but never fired (0 runs), so I hand in this agent instead.

Tricky case & fix

The case: NEA has no PSI reading

When data.gov.sg has no PSI data for the time asked, it does not send an empty list. It answers {"code":17, "name":"REAL_TIME_API_DATA_NOT_FOUND", "data":null}. I simulated this by asking for a date with no data (psi?date=2030-01-01) in a draft of the Zap, so the live Zap was never touched.

What I expected, written before the run (Tue 20 Oct 2026, 01:16 SGT)

  • The code step reads psiRes.data.items[0] with no guard, so it should throw a TypeError ("Cannot read properties of null").
  • The run should show as Errored at step 3, step 4 should never run, and no email should be sent.
  • The weather part is already protected but the PSI part is not, so one missing feed stops the whole update, weather included.

What happened (test run 01:18:20 SGT)

Exactly as expected: Errored at step 3 with TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'items') at line 3, and step 4 Skipped. No email was sent. In real use I would get silence, and only Zap history would show why.

The fix (v3)

  • The PSI request now has .catch(() => null) and is read with optional chaining (psiRes?.data?.items?.[0]), so a missing or broken feed cannot crash the step.
  • A hasPsi check confirms all five regions have numbers. If not, the email still goes out with the subject "Singapore PSI unavailable", NEA's own message ("Data not found") and the full weather section.
  • The PSI request sits in one PSI_PATH line, so the case can be replayed by changing that one line.

The rerun of the same case (test run 01:21:34 SGT)

Successful, all four steps. The email arrived with the subject "Singapore PSI unavailable | 28.8°C, Thundery Showers - 20 Oct 2026, 1:21 am" and the body "No PSI reading available from NEA right now (data.gov.sg said: Data not found). The next update will try again in 6 hours.", followed by the weather. Then I set PSI_PATH back to live data, tested the step (a normal email with highest PSI 117), published v3 and checked that the live code no longer has the test date.

Q1 · Where does my agent sit, and what does that mean for the person in the loop?

(a) My quadrant

AI alone, with checks (bottom left).

(b) The two questions

Work with a right answer. Every number in the email has a source of truth: NEA's published reading for that hour. I did not need any judgement to mark the emails right. For three scheduled runs, all 30 numbers matched NEA's record exactly. The labels are also fixed rules, not opinions: "Unhealthy" means PSI 101 to 200 on NEA's scale. Even the tricky case had a right answer: "there is no reading" is a fact, and after the fix the email says exactly that instead of guessing or going silent.

Work that either of them can do alone. I could look up the PSI and the forecast myself in a minute, and the Zap sent 22 scheduled emails with no person inside any run, with no approval step between the fetch and the email. Nothing in the job needs a person and AI working on the same item at the same time.

(c) The person in the loop

Where I stay at the gate: after the run, not before. Once a day I (1) check that the Zap is still on, (2) scan Zap history for anything Errored, because before the fix a missing feed produced no email at all, so my inbox alone could not show a failure, and (3) compare one email with NEA's figure for the same hour. What the agent may do alone: read three public, read-only NEA feeds, work out averages and the official bands, and email one fixed address, mine. What would move it: if it gave advice that depends on the person, such as "cancel the outdoor sports day" or "staff should work from home", the job would have no single right answer and would move to A person decides, AI alongside, with a manager making the call. If it sent haze alerts to other people (staff, parents or clients) who act on them, a wrong number would have a real cost, so I would sign off each alert before it goes out. That moves it to A person leads, AI inside.

(d) My group's view

Our group's board (board.html) puts the task closest to mine, Stocks: pull daily closing prices, in AI alone, with checks, because it is "a fixed, checkable fact" that a script can fetch alone with a spot check. I agree, and my agent belongs right beside it. I also agree with putting the agent-style tasks (screening stocks for a watchlist, gathering sources for a literature review) in Agents run it, a person judges, because there is no single right list.

Where I would answer differently is the together or alone question. The group answered it by asking whether a person must be inside each run. My tricky case shows that "alone" still needs a named person outside the run: when the feed had no data, the agent could not notice, sent nothing, and the failure showed only in Zap history. So I keep my agent in "alone", but I would add that every "alone" agent needs someone who checks that it is still running and not failing silently, not only that its answers are right. I would also be stricter on the right answer question. My agent's "Unhealthy" label is a right answer only because NEA fixes the bands. The moment it turns that into advice ("wear a mask today"), the right answer depends on the person (asthma, age, plans), and it moves to the right-hand column.

What I checked, and howEach version of the code was run against live NEA data before it went into Zapier, and the code in Zapier was compared character by character (by a checksum) with the tested file. The filter was tested with real trigger records (a 2 pm record must stop, a midnight record must pass). The three emails were checked against NEA's historical API, and Zap history was scanned for errors. After the tricky case, the published code was checked to confirm it uses the live PSI request, not the test date. Email addresses are covered in every screenshot, and *.json is in .gitignore, so the Zap-history file stays off GitHub.
One AI error I caughtThe AI's first filter used Zapier's (Text) Is in rule with "0,6,12". The filter test showed the 2 pm record would pass, because "Is in" matches text inside text, and "2" is inside "12". Published like that, the Zap would have emailed at 1, 2, 6 and 12 o'clock, am and pm, about eight emails a day, over Zapier's limit of 5. It was replaced with three "Exactly matches" rules joined by OR and tested again (2 pm blocked). A smaller one: the AI assumed midnight would show as hour "0", but a real record showed "12". That was harmless, because 12 was already allowed.

Reflection

My agent fitted the job well: reading official, checkable numbers on a fixed schedule and emailing them only to me is narrow, right-answer work it did alone, with 22 scheduled emails, no errors and 30 of 30 checked numbers matching NEA. It failed where its data source went quiet: when NEA had no PSI reading, the code crashed and no email went out, so silence looked the same as clean air, and it also needed paid Zapier features that the brief asked me to avoid. I would keep the "PSI unavailable" fallback, add a daily "still running" check, and before building, test whether the job fits one trigger and one action on the free plan, rather than finding out after.
